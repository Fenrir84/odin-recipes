Lasagna

Description

This is a delicious comfort food staple

Ingredients

  • 15 oz. ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 3/4 cup Parmesan cheese, freshly grated

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil over medium heat and add the diced onions.
  2. Add the ground beef and sausage and increase heat to medium-high.
  3. Add the chicken broth and use a silocone spatula to clean the bottom and sides of the pot.
